Privacy Policy

Kyle Local Multipost · Last updated: 2026-08-15 · Operator-only tool (no public signup)

Who we are

Kyle Local Multipost (“the App”) is a personal scheduler used by the operator to publish content to the operator’s own social accounts (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, and related platforms). There is no multi-tenant public product and no signup for third parties.

Data we process

  • Account tokens and IDs required to publish to platforms the operator connects (e.g. Meta Page / Instagram Business or Creator).
  • Content the operator chooses to schedule (captions, media files, schedule times).
  • Technical logs needed to operate delivery (success/failure receipts). Logs do not include passwords or raw app secrets in exportable form.

What we do not do

  • No public user accounts or open registration.
  • No selling personal data.
  • No posting as people other than the authenticated operator’s own brands.

Where data lives

Primary storage is on the operator’s own machines / private infrastructure. Platform APIs receive only what is required to authenticate and publish the operator’s content.

Third parties

When the operator connects Meta (or other networks), those platforms process data under their own policies. The App only uses official APIs for the operator’s connected accounts.

Retention

Tokens and content packages are retained only as long as needed for scheduling, delivery receipts, and operator audit. The operator may revoke platform access and delete local data at any time.

Deletion and revocation

The operator can revoke a connected platform account through that platform’s native settings. Requests concerning App-held content or delivery records may be sent to the role address below. The request will be matched to the connected account before deletion.
